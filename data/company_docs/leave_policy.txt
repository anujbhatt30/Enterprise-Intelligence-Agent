Company Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
accrued monthly at 1.5 days/month. Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum
of 10 days into the next year. Leave must be requested at least 3 working days in advance
through the HR portal, except in emergencies.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required
for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry forward.

3. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver. Paternity leave is
2 weeks of paid leave for the secondary caregiver, to be taken within 6 months of the
child's birth or adoption.

4. Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote
arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

5. Public Holidays
Employees are entitled to all government-declared public holidays applicable to their
work location, in addition to annual leave.
